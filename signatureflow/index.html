SignatureFlow

Privacy Policy

Effective August 19, 2026

SignatureFlow lets you sign and annotate documents on your device. This Privacy Policy explains how SignatureFlow handles information when you use the app.

Our approach

Your data is designed to stay under your control. SignatureFlow does not sell personal information, does not use it for cross-app advertising, and does not build advertising profiles.

Documents and signatures

Documents you open, the signatures you draw, and the signed files you produce are processed and stored locally on your device. SignatureFlow does not upload your documents to our servers.

Camera and photos

SignatureFlow requests camera or photo library access only when you choose to import or scan a document. Those images remain on your device unless you export or share them yourself.

iCloud synchronization

iCloud synchronization is optional. If you turn it on, SignatureFlow synchronizes the data needed for that feature through your private iCloud database using Apple CloudKit. Apple handles that service under its own privacy policy and account settings. You can turn synchronization off in SignatureFlow or in your device settings.

Subscriptions and purchases

SignatureFlow purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. SignatureFlow receives the subscription or purchase status needed to unlock the features you bought. We do not receive or store your full payment-card information.

Analytics and tracking

SignatureFlow does not include third-party advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can manage app permissions in your device settings, disable iCloud synchronization, and delete your locally stored SignatureFlow data by removing it in the app or deleting the app. Data previously synchronized to iCloud may also need to be managed through your Apple account and iCloud settings.

Security

We use platform security features and reasonable safeguards designed to protect information handled by SignatureFlow. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and you should keep your device and Apple Account secure.

Children's privacy

SignatureFlow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as SignatureFlow changes. When we do, we will update the effective date above.
