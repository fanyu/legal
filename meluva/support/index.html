Sleep support, privacy questions, and feedback

Need help with Meluva? Email support@meluva.app.

Include these details

Tell us which Meluva feature you were using, your iPhone and Apple Watch model if relevant, and your iOS or watchOS version. Please do not send health records, audio clips, passwords, or other sensitive information unless it is necessary and you are comfortable sharing it.

Permissions and device settings

Meluva's sleep features can depend on HealthKit, microphone, notification, and alarm permissions. You can review or change permissions in iOS Settings. If an Apple Watch feature is unavailable, check that your watch is paired, charged, and running a supported version of watchOS.

Privacy

Read the Meluva Privacy Policy to learn how the app handles sleep data, sound-event monitoring, and user-initiated exports.
