Meluva

Privacy Policy

Effective September 3, 2026

Meluva is an adult wellness sleep companion for iPhone and Apple Watch. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information. Meluva is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent a medical condition.

Our approach

Meluva is designed to work without a Meluva account or a developer-operated data server. We do not sell personal information, use it for cross-app advertising, or build advertising profiles.

Sleep and health information

If you grant permission, Meluva reads the HealthKit data needed for the sleep features you enable. Sleep episodes, measurements, reports, trends, and tags are stored on your device. We do not receive that information on a Meluva server or disclose it to advertisers.

Microphone and sound-event monitoring

If you choose to enable sound-event monitoring, Meluva processes sound on your iPhone. Full-night raw audio is not uploaded to us. Short clips for selected sound events may be kept locally on your device so that you can review or delete them.

Alarms, notifications, and Apple Watch

Meluva uses iOS and watchOS features to schedule alarms, deliver notifications, show status, and coordinate with your Apple Watch when you enable those features. These actions are handled by your devices and Apple services; they do not require sending your sleep data to a Meluva server.

Exports and sharing

Meluva may let you create an export of selected sleep information. An export is created only when you choose it and review what is included. If you share an export with another app, service, or person, that recipient's privacy practices apply to the information you send.

Analytics and tracking

Meluva does not include third-party advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can manage HealthKit, microphone, notification, and other permissions in iOS Settings. You can delete Meluva records and locally retained sound-event clips in the app where available, or remove the app and its local data from your device.

Security

Meluva relies on platform security features and reasonable safeguards designed to protect information handled on your device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so keep your device and Apple Account secure.

Children's privacy

Meluva is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Meluva changes. When we do, we will update the effective date above.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact support@meluva.app or visit the Meluva Support page.
