PasteFlow

Privacy Policy

Effective August 19, 2026

PasteFlow turns content you copy into the next action. This Privacy Policy explains how PasteFlow handles information when you use the app.

Our approach

Your data is designed to stay under your control. PasteFlow does not sell personal information, does not use it for cross-app advertising, and does not build advertising profiles.

Clipboard content

PasteFlow reads clipboard content only when you invoke the app or a PasteFlow action. Clipboard items and any history PasteFlow keeps are stored locally on your device. PasteFlow does not transmit clipboard content to us.

Actions and external services

Some actions open, search, or send the copied content to a destination that you choose, such as a website, a search engine, or another app. When you run such an action, the content you selected and standard network information, including your IP address, reach that destination. Those services handle the request under their own policies.

iCloud synchronization

iCloud synchronization is optional. If you turn it on, PasteFlow synchronizes the data needed for that feature through your private iCloud database using Apple CloudKit. Apple handles that service under its own privacy policy and account settings. You can turn synchronization off in PasteFlow or in your device settings.

Subscriptions and purchases

PasteFlow purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. PasteFlow receives the subscription or purchase status needed to unlock the features you bought. We do not receive or store your full payment-card information.

Analytics and tracking

PasteFlow does not include third-party advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can manage app permissions in your device settings, disable iCloud synchronization, and delete your locally stored PasteFlow data by removing it in the app or deleting the app. Data previously synchronized to iCloud may also need to be managed through your Apple account and iCloud settings.

Security

We use platform security features and reasonable safeguards designed to protect information handled by PasteFlow. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and you should keep your device and Apple Account secure.

Children's privacy

PasteFlow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as PasteFlow changes. When we do, we will update the effective date above.
