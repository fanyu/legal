PasteFlow

Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

PasteFlow turns content you copy into the next action. This Privacy Policy explains how PasteFlow handles information when you use the macOS app. Clipboard data stays on your Mac. PasteFlow does not provide iCloud sync and does not operate a developer server that collects clipboard content.

Our approach

Your data is designed to stay under your control. PasteFlow does not sell personal information, does not use it for cross-app advertising, and does not build advertising profiles.

Clipboard content

While PasteFlow is running, it can read the system pasteboard to monitor and save clipboard history according to your settings. This may include text, images, file references, links, timestamps, content type, and favorite or pin state. History, snippets, settings, actions, workflows, and per-app routing rules are stored in the app's local data on your Mac. PasteFlow does not transmit your clipboard history to us.

Local storage and sensitive content

PasteFlow provides local exclusion rules, sensitive-content expiration, confirmation before external actions, and backup-exclusion controls. If you enable local encryption, sensitive text is encrypted on your Mac using a key stored in the system Keychain. This does not mean that all ordinary clipboard history is encrypted.

No cloud sync

The current version does not provide iCloud or other multi-device synchronization. PasteFlow does not upload clipboard history, snippets, settings, or workflows to the cloud.

Backups and imports

PasteFlow creates a backup only when you choose a destination and confirm the export. JSON backup files are not encrypted. Sensitive items stored through local encrypted references are always omitted because their device-only keys and payloads are not portable. Other sensitive items follow your backup-exclusion setting, and PasteFlow warns you before exporting sensitive content in plaintext. You are responsible for protecting exported backup files.

Actions and external services

Some actions open, search, or send the copied content to a destination that you choose, such as a website, a search engine, or another app. When you run such an action, the content you selected and standard network information, including your IP address, reach that destination. Those services handle the request under their own policies.

Purchases and feedback

PasteFlow Pro is a one-time App Store purchase. Apple processes payment information, and the app receives only StoreKit transaction state needed to complete or restore access. Public GitHub feedback includes the report you enter and app version, build, macOS version, language, and timestamp metadata. Email feedback is handled by your configured mail provider. Do not submit confidential clipboard content, passwords, tokens, or other sensitive information.

Analytics and tracking

PasteFlow does not include third-party advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can manage app permissions in macOS, configure capture and sensitive-content controls, clear history in PasteFlow, and delete locally stored app data. Exported backups, public GitHub issues, sent email, and content sent to an external destination are outside PasteFlow's local deletion controls.

Security

We use platform security features and reasonable safeguards designed to protect information handled by PasteFlow. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and you should keep your device and Apple Account secure.

Children's privacy

PasteFlow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as PasteFlow changes. When we do, we will update the effective date above.
