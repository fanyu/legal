Flow: Expense & Bill Tracker

For app issues, general feedback, and feature requests, contact us by email. We welcome messages in any language.

support@flowwish.app

For the fastest help, include your Flow version, iOS version, device model, and the steps that reproduce the issue.

Before you write

  • Do not include passwords, payment-card details, account numbers, or other sensitive financial information.
  • If the issue involves a subscription, include the Apple Account receipt or transaction identifier only when Apple Support has asked you to provide it.
  • For data or synchronization issues, describe whether you use local storage or optional iCloud synchronization.
