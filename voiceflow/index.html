VoiceFlow

Privacy Policy

Effective August 19, 2026

VoiceFlow is a voice capture and transcription app. This Privacy Policy explains how VoiceFlow handles information when you use the app.

Our approach

Your data is designed to stay under your control. VoiceFlow does not sell personal information, does not use it for cross-app advertising, and does not build advertising profiles.

Microphone, recordings, and transcription

VoiceFlow requests microphone access only when you choose to record. Recordings and transcripts are stored locally on your device. Speech recognition may be performed on-device or, where the system speech service requires it, by Apple under its own privacy policy.

Optional AI processing

If you connect and enable an AI provider that you configure, the text or audio you submit for that feature is sent to that provider to generate a response. Review the provider's privacy policy before enabling an external AI connection.

iCloud synchronization

iCloud synchronization is optional. If you turn it on, VoiceFlow synchronizes the data needed for that feature through your private iCloud database using Apple CloudKit. Apple handles that service under its own privacy policy and account settings. You can turn synchronization off in VoiceFlow or in your device settings.

Subscriptions and purchases

VoiceFlow purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. VoiceFlow receives the subscription or purchase status needed to unlock the features you bought. We do not receive or store your full payment-card information.

Analytics and tracking

VoiceFlow does not include third-party advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can manage app permissions in your device settings, disable iCloud synchronization, and delete your locally stored VoiceFlow data by removing it in the app or deleting the app. Data previously synchronized to iCloud may also need to be managed through your Apple account and iCloud settings.

Security

We use platform security features and reasonable safeguards designed to protect information handled by VoiceFlow. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and you should keep your device and Apple Account secure.

Children's privacy

VoiceFlow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as VoiceFlow changes. When we do, we will update the effective date above.
