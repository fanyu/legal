Reverie

Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

Reverie turns selected photos into tactile digital albums. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

On-device by design

Reverie does not require an account and does not operate a server that receives your albums or photos. Album layouts, titles, notes, shelf settings, and on-device analysis results remain on your device. We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or track you across apps or websites.

Photos

With your permission, Reverie reads the photos you select from your system photo library to display and arrange them. Reverie does not modify or delete originals in your photo library. By default, albums keep references to the originals. If you enable “Keep Photo Copies,” the app stores copies inside its local app container so selected album photos can remain available if an original is later removed. You can disable this option and delete those local copies.

On-device photo analysis

If enabled, Reverie uses Apple system frameworks on your device to organize selected album photos, identify utility images such as screenshots or receipts, choose representative photos, and group recurring faces without identifying who a person is. Photos and analysis results are not uploaded to us. Disabling analysis deletes its saved results.

Notifications and widgets

If you grant permission, Reverie may schedule local memory notifications. Its widget reads a limited album-shelf summary and locally rendered cover images from an App Group container shared only by Reverie and its widget. These features do not send album content to a developer-operated server.

Purchases

Reverie Pro purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Reverie receives verified product and entitlement status from StoreKit but does not receive or store your payment-card details.

Analytics and tracking

Reverie contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs and does not create an advertising identifier.

Your choices and deletion

You can change photo and notification permissions in iOS Settings. You can remove albums, local photo copies, and on-device analysis results in the app. Deleting Reverie removes data in its app container; original photos in the system photo library remain under your control.

Children's privacy

Reverie is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as Reverie changes. We will update the effective date above when we do.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact edison.support@icloud.com or visit Reverie Support.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 24 日

Reverie 将你选择的照片编排成具有实体感的数字相册。本政策说明 App 如何处理你的信息。

默认仅在本机处理

Reverie 不要求注册账号，也没有接收你的相册或照片的开发者服务器。相册编排、标题、笔记、相册架设置和本机分析结果保存在设备上。我们不会出售个人信息、用于广告或跨 App 与网站追踪你。

照片

获得授权后，Reverie 读取你从系统照片图库中选择的照片，用于展示和编排，不会修改或删除图库原图。默认情况下，相册保存原图引用；打开“保留照片副本”后，App 会在本机容器保存副本。你可以关闭该功能并删除这些副本。

本机照片分析

打开后，Reverie 使用 Apple 系统框架在设备上整理已入册照片、排除截图或票据等工具类图片、挑选代表照片，并在不识别身份的前提下对重复出现的人脸分组。照片和分析结果不会上传；关闭分析会删除已有结果。

通知、小组件与购买

经你授权，Reverie 可安排本地回忆通知。小组件只通过 Reverie 与其小组件专用的 App Group 读取有限的相册架摘要和本机封面图片。Reverie Pro 由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理；App 不接触或保存银行卡信息。

分析、追踪与删除

Reverie 不包含第三方广告、分析或跨 App 追踪 SDK。你可以在 iOS 设置中调整照片与通知权限，并在 App 中删除相册、照片副本和分析结果。删除 App 会移除其本机容器中的数据，不会删除系统照片图库的原图。

儿童、更新与联系

Reverie 并非面向 13 岁以下儿童。政策更新时会修改上方生效日期。如有隐私问题，请联系 edison.support@icloud.com，或访问 Reverie 支持页面。
