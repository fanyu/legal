Support

Albums, purchases, privacy, and feedback

Need help with Reverie? Email edison.support@icloud.com.

What to include

Tell us what you were doing, your iPhone or iPad model, the iOS or iPadOS version, and the Reverie version shown in Settings. Please do not send original photos, payment information, passwords, or other sensitive material.

Photos and permissions

You can review Reverie's photo and notification permissions in iOS Settings. Reverie never modifies or deletes originals in the system photo library. If an album photo becomes unavailable, confirm that the original still exists and that Reverie still has permission to access it.

Reverie Pro

Purchases are handled by Apple. If a completed purchase is not recognized, use “Restore Purchases” in Reverie and confirm that the device is signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Privacy and terms

Read the Reverie Privacy Policy and Apple's Standard End User License Agreement.
